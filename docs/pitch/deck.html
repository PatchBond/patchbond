The idea

Make wasting a maintainer's time cost something.

1 · Bond

Strangers attach a small refundable deposit

USDC or NEAR — each repo chooses. Maintainers, collaborators and proven contributors pay $0.

2 · Verifiable AI

A TEE model judges it after CI

Using facts we compute. The response is signed and public.

3 · Settle

Refund — or the repo is paid

Real work: every cent back. Slop: 90% to the repo's vault.

The opposite of tea.xyz: spam costs money instead of earning it. No token. Nobody is paid for submitting.

PatchBond4
The product

One comment on the PR. One tap to pay.

The GitHub App asks strangers for a bond with the live price and a 24 h deadline. They pay from any NEAR wallet — USDC gasless (NEP-366), or NEAR wrapped in the same transaction.

The maintainer gets a GitHub email when it's paid. After CI, the verdict lands on the PR as a check and a comment.

The judge

Facts first. Then a verifiable model.

CIThe repo's own checks — we never run contributor code
Phantom codeCalls into the project's own namespace that don't exist
Claims vs diff“Added tests”, “updated README” — is it in the diff?
AssignmentWas the issue assigned to someone else?
InjectionText that tries to instruct the judge
Malicious codeHidden Unicode, decoded-payload exec, curl | sh, secret exfiltration, poisoned workflows

A forfeit needs high confidence, evidence and a hard fact. When in doubt, refund.

Fair to both sides

Nobody can game it — not even the maintainer.

Contributors protected

A maintainer can't keep your bond on their own

Their forfeit is a 48 h proposal you dispute for free. A stronger model re-judges. Overturned? You get your bond back plus a penalty from their vault — and it's on their public record.

Attacks caught

Malicious code forfeits automatically

A deterministic scan — not the model's mood — finds Trojan-Source Unicode, hidden payloads, install-time downloads and poisoned CI. Still challengeable. Zero false hits on 30 real PRs.

Attention kept

Unpaid PRs cost nothing to ignore

Labelled deposit paid / awaiting, closed after an announced 24 h, reopened the moment they pay.
