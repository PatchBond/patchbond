The idea

Make wasting a maintainer's time cost something.

1 · Bond

Strangers attach a small refundable deposit

USDC or NEAR — each repo chooses. Maintainers, collaborators and proven contributors pay $0.

2 · Verifiable AI

A TEE model judges it after CI

Using facts we compute. The response is signed and public.

3 · Settle

Refund — or the repo is paid

Real work: every cent back. Slop: 90% to the repo's vault.

The bond sits in a NEAR contract — never with us; only the repo's linked payout account can withdraw. The opposite of tea.xyz: spam costs money instead of earning it. No token.

The product

One comment on the PR. One tap to pay.

The GitHub App asks strangers for a bond with the live price and a 24 h deadline. They pay from any NEAR wallet — USDC gasless (NEP-366), or NEAR wrapped in the same transaction.

The maintainer gets a GitHub email when it's paid. After CI, the verdict lands on the PR as a check and a comment.

Fair to both sides

Protects contributors, maintainers and the code.

Contributors protected

A maintainer can't keep your bond on their own

Their forfeit is a 48 h proposal you dispute for free. A stronger model re-judges. Overturned? You get your bond back plus a penalty from their vault — and it's on their public record.

Attacks caught

Malicious code is flagged before anyone reviews it

A deterministic scan finds Trojan-Source Unicode, hidden payloads, install-time downloads and poisoned CI, and proposes a forfeit. It settles on-chain only if the model signed a forfeit too — otherwise a maintainer confirms. No false alarms on 50 real PRs (none of them was malicious — real attack samples are next).

Attention kept

Unpaid PRs cost nothing to ignore

Labelled deposit paid / awaiting, closed after an announced 24 h, reopened the moment they pay.

Not a slop filter — a market

Four mechanisms, one NEAR contract.

Priority lane

Pay for attention, not for submitting

A review tip reaches the repo only if a maintainer reviews within the promised window — otherwise it's refunded.

Optimistic verdicts

Cheap AI by default, strong AI when money's on the line

Stake to challenge; a stronger model re-judges with both sides' statements; the loser's stake pays the winner.

Slop funds the fix

Spam pays whoever really fixes the issue

A forfeited PR that claimed to fix #N becomes a bounty on #N, paid when a real fix is merged.

Know your agent

Capped wallets for coding agents

A function-call key that can only pay bonds, under a daily cap the contract enforces; a public record per harness. On testnet, the bond over the cap was refused.
